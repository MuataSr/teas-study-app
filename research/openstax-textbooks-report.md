## TEAS Coverage Map

### Reading (28% of exam)
- No dedicated OpenStax textbook needed
- Coverage approach: exam strategy and practice questions rather than content study
- Key skills tested: identifying main ideas, summarizing, following directions, interpreting graphics

### Mathematics (26% of exam)
- **Numbers & Operations:** Prealgebra 2e (Ch. 1–4)
- **Algebra:** Prealgebra 2e (Ch. 5–9) + Elementary Algebra 2e (Ch. 1–5)
- **Measurement & Data:** Introductory Statistics 2e (Ch. 1–2 for basic concepts)
- **Pre-algebra content is sufficient** for ~80% of TEAS Math; Elementary Algebra covers the rest

### Science (32% of exam — highest weight)
- **Human Anatomy & Physiology (≈70% of Science):** Anatomy and Physiology 2e — primary resource
- **Life & Physical Sciences (≈15%):** Concepts of Biology + Chemistry 2e
- **Scientific Reasoning (≈15%):** Practice and strategy, no specific textbook needed

### English & Language Usage (14% of exam)
- Writing Guide with Handbook covers grammar, sentence structure, punctuation, vocabulary
- No separate vocabulary textbook needed — TEAS tests contextual word meaning, not memorization

## TEAS Coverage Gaps

1. **Reading Comprehension Strategies** — No OpenStax textbook teaches how to identify main ideas, make inferences, or follow multi-step directions. This is typically covered by exam prep materials, not content textbooks.

2. **Scientific Reasoning** — OpenStax textbooks teach science content, not how to evaluate hypotheses or interpret scientific studies. This gap is best filled with practice questions.

3. **TEAS-Specific Math Word Problems** — OpenStax algebra textbooks teach concepts but don't include the multi-step word problem format that TEAS uses. Practice questions needed.

4. **No College-Level Reading Textbook** — OpenStax has no reading comprehension textbook. The "College Reading and Writing" content was folded into the Writing Guide with Handbook, which focuses on writing, not reading comprehension.

## Recommendations

- **Primary study path:** A&P 2e (Science) → Prealgebra + Elementary Algebra (Math) → Writing Guide (English)
- **Secondary resources:** Biology and Chemistry for science depth, Statistics for data interpretation questions
- **Gaps to fill with custom content:** Reading strategies, scientific reasoning, TEAS-format practice problems
