## 2. Smart Edition Nursing

**Popular third-party option.** Strong brand in the prenursing Reddit/community space.

### Pricing

| Product | Price |
|---------|-------|
| Online Course (1 month) | **$39.99/mo** |
| Online Course (3 months) | **$79.99** |
| Study Guide (book) | ~$30-40 (Amazon) |
| Online Course + Printed Guide bundle | Higher tier |

### Features
- 8 practice tests
- 1,500+ questions with video explanations
- 100+ educational videos (Math, Reading, English, Science)
- Companion mobile app (free practice test, paid full course)
- Flashcards
- "Guaranteed to pass" promise

**Key strength:** Good value at $79.99/3mo. Video explanations for every question. Active community following.

**Key weakness:** Still quiz-based, not AI-tutoring. No adaptive learning like SmartPrep. Subscription model.

## 3. Pocket Prep

**Mobile-first.** The "study in line at the grocery store" app. 135+ exam types.

### Pricing

| Plan | Price |
|------|-------|
| Basic (free) | $0 (limited questions) |
| Paid | **$15-20/mo** (exact tiers vary) |

### Features
- 2,000 TEAS questions
- 3 full-length mock exams
- "Pass Guarantee"
- iOS + Android + desktop
- Question of the day
- Custom quizzes by subject

**Key strength:** Dead simple UX. Perfect for micro-study sessions. Low monthly cost.

**Key weakness:** Shallow content compared to ATI/Smart Edition. No video lessons. No study guide. Users report seeing repeat questions. Not comprehensive enough as sole prep source.

## 6. Cheaper Alternatives (App Store Scattered)

Several smaller apps exist on iOS/Android with weekly/monthly subs ($8-12/wk, $17-25/mo). These tend to have:
- 500-1,500 questions
- No study guides or video lessons
- Questionable quality (Reddit complaints about repeated questions, inaccurate content)
- No brand recognition

**Not worth tracking as serious competitors.**

## Market Gap Analysis — Where Mu2 Wins

### What EVERY competitor does
Static multiple-choice quizzes + answer explanations. That's it. Some add video lessons or flashcards. None offer **real-time AI tutoring**.

### What NONE of them do
- **Socratic tutoring** — ask "why is this formula used?" and get a guided explanation, not a canned paragraph
- **Adaptive conversation** — the app responds to *your* specific confusion, not a pre-written script
- **Conceptual depth** — students can drill into WHY, not just WHETHER they got it right
- **AI-powered** — every competitor is a static quiz bank; Mu2 uses cloud AI (Gemma 4 E4B + 26B) for real-time tutoring
- **PWA** — works like a native app from the browser, no app store download required

### Mu2's Differentiator
**AI Socratic Tutor vs. Static Quiz Dump**

Every competitor sells the same thing: question banks with explanations. Mu2 sells a *tutor that talks back*. The dual-agent harness (Gemma 4 E4B router + 26B A4B teacher) means:
1. Student asks a question or gets stuck
2. Router identifies the concept
3. Teacher explains it Socratically — guides, doesn't lecture
4. Student learns *why*, not just *what*

This is the "filing cabinet gets smarter" philosophy in action. The KB compounds. The model stays the same. The harness delivers what no quiz app can.

### Pricing Sweet Spot
Competitors range $20-200. Mu2's target: **$29-49 one-time** or **$9.99/mo** subscription. Undercut everyone while delivering a fundamentally better experience.
