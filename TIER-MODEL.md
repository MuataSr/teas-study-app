# Mu2 TEAS Study App — Tier Model

## Free Tier (Open Source)

**"The best free TEAS prep that exists."**

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| 2,003 MCQs | Math, Science, Reading, English — real questions with full explanations |
| Smart Question Selection | Serves more questions from areas the student keeps missing |
| Weak Spots Dashboard | Top 5 struggling topics, updated after every session |
| Progress Heatmap | "Up 40% in linear equations, haven't touched geometry in 2 weeks" |
| Spaced Repetition | Wrong answers come back at intervals, weighted by miss frequency |
| Quickfire Mode | Rapid-fire timed quiz for exam-day stamina |
| Confidence Slider | Self-assess per question, track confidence vs accuracy |
| Stats & History | Full session history, accuracy trends, time-per-question |
| Dark Mode | Study at 2 AM without burning retinas |

**Trace Memory is active in the free tier.** The student sees it working — their dashboard adapts, their quizzes adapt, their progress data is *theirs* and visible. This is not hidden data collection. It is the product.

## AI Tutor Tier (Premium)

**"A tutor who's been reading your chart."**

Everything in Free, plus:

| Feature | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|

## The Rollout Flow

**Week 1-3: Student uses Free tier**
- Quizzes, tracks progress, sees their Weak Spots dashboard evolve
- App is genuinely useful — better than most paid alternatives
- Zero mention of AI. Not in the UI, not in the marketing.

**Week 3-4: The Reveal**
- Student gets a personalized report: *"Here's everything we've learned about how you study."*
- Shareable. Brag-worthy. TikTok-ready.
- One button: *"Meet your tutor."*

**The AI tutor activates warm — not cold.**
- It has 2-3 weeks of trace data
- First message references the student's actual patterns
- The gap between free and premium is *felt*, not described

---

## The Key Principle

**Free tier is a real product. AI tutor is the upgrade.**

If a student never pays, they still have the best free TEAS tool available. The trace data serves them *openly* in the free tier. The AI tutor simply uses that same data to go deeper.

No bait. No switch. No shady data collection.
